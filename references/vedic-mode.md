# Vedic Mode

Use this mode when the user asks for a Vedic, Jyotish, or nakshatra-based
reading. It applies this skill's psychological-archetypal register to the
structures of Jyotish — it is *not* a traditional Jyotish delivery (no
remedies, no gemstones, no muhurta, no event prediction), and it says so
lightly if asked. The restraint rules of `synthesis.md` §5 apply in full;
dashas in particular are described as seasons of emphasis, never timed
predictions.

## Conventions

Run the calculator with `--vedic`, which sets sidereal (Lahiri) + whole-sign
houses and enriches the output with the nakshatra (name, pada, deity, symbol)
of every placement and angle, the lagna lord by *traditional* rulership
(Mars owns Scorpio, Saturn owns Aquarius, Jupiter owns Pisces — no outer
planets as lords), and the Vimshottari mahadasha timeline. Outer planets may
still be interpreted as psychological voices, but they rule nothing here.

## How to read (in order)

1. **Lagna and lagna lord.** The rising sign is the vessel; the lagna lord's
   sign, house, and nakshatra are the vessel's captain and course. A lagna
   lord in the 9th aims the life at dharma; in the 2nd at value and speech;
   and so on — houses keep their lexicon meanings, with the classical accents
   (9th = fortune/dharma, 8th = depth/transformation, read
   developmentally, never as doom).
2. **The nakshatras — the mythic layer.** This is what Vedic mode uniquely
   offers this skill: twenty-seven mansions, each with a deity and a symbol,
   sitting beneath the twelve signs like an older stratum of story. Give the
   Moon's, Sun's, and Ascendant's mansions full mythic treatment — tell the
   deity's story properly, per the amplification rules in voice.md — and any
   other mansion that a lead planet occupies. When one mansion holds two of
   the tripod (Sun, Moon, ASC), it names the chart's presiding myth: give it
   the title role.
3. **Dignities, lightly.** Exaltation, own sign, and debilitation are worth
   naming when they touch a lead planet (exalted Saturn is an engineer at
   full strength; debilitated planets are named with the same dignity as any
   shadow — a voice working against the grain of its room, which is a
   developmental task, not a defect). Skip the full shadbala apparatus.
4. **Dashas as chapters.** Present the mahadasha sequence as the life's
   seasons: which member of the parliament holds the gavel, from when to
   when, and what quality of attention that season rewards. Interpret the
   dasha lord *as placed in this chart* (its house, sign, nakshatra). Name
   the current season and the next transition year; never attach predicted
   events to either.
5. **Convergence with the tropical chart.** If a tropical reading exists for
   the same person, close with what both skies agree on — houses and aspects
   barely move, so agreements (same house Moon, identical tight aspects,
   nodal direction) carry doubled weight per the dual-zodiac rules. Sign
   shifts between the two zodiacs are octaves, not contradictions; the
   zodiac-modes.md chord rule applies.

## Voice

Identical register to the rest of the skill — second person, mythically
literate, dignity-preserving — but the metaphor palette draws from the
nakshatra deities and symbols themselves, which are the richest archetypal
material this mode owns. The dialect rule still applies: the chart's dominant
element tunes the music. A Vedic reading of a fire chart still burns; the
mansions just give it older lamps.
