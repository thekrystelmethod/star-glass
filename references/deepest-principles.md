# Deepest Principles — the map

This is the structural half of the lexicon. `lexicon.md` fixes what each symbol
*is*; this fixes how the symbols **produce each other**. Where the two touch,
this file governs.

The frame comes from the *Astrology For The Soul* free course (Moses Siregar III,
c. 2005) — Exercises 1 through 5, the six-axis page, and the elements page —
read against Krystel's own model of light and shadow. Nothing here is a style
rule. It is comprehension: the tether that makes reading a shadow legitimate
rather than assigning one.

---

## 1. Light is primary. Shadow is blocked access.

A sign's shadow is **not** the gift overrun, **not** the armour the gift was
built as, and **not** the opposite sign's territory. It is that sign's own
light, unlived, because access to it was blocked while the person was young or
vulnerable — often by someone who loved them and did not mean to, who stepped on
the thing without noticing it was there.

**The urge toward shadow is made of the lack.** Shadow is hunger, not evil, and
never a moral failing.

So the derivation runs one direction only:

> name what this placement's light actually is → ask what a child does when
> *that specific light* is unavailable → write what you find there

Never reach for a stock darkness. Two different charts must never arrive at the
same shadow. Describe the child's experience of early care without indicting the
parent; do not manufacture a villain in order to explain a wound.

Blockage is recorded in the geometry by **weighted hard aspects, the nodal axis,
and Saturn.**

---

## 2. Planets carry the weight

Houses and signs are the noun and the manner. A planet is a body with *gravitas*
— the importance of any region of a chart comes from the planetary mass standing
in it. "Weighted" always means mass and tight orb, never house symbolism on its
own.

An empty house still has its principle in full. It simply has no weight.

---

## 3. The horizon, and the six spines

Houses 1–6 lie below the horizon: the self, the roots. Houses 7–12 lie above it:
the world, the branches. **Roots before branches.** Each upper house is the
output of the lower house facing it.

| the branch (wanted) | the root (built) |
|---|---|
| 7th — to be met, chosen, partnered | 1st — **self**. Nobody can be met as a draft. |
| 8th — deep healing, intimacy, being changed | 2nd — **self-love**, self-worth |
| 9th — meaning, faith, the long view | 3rd — **study**, the daily mind |
| 10th — standing, being seen | 4th — **soul**, the private ground |
| 11th — the commons, a current larger than you | 5th — **self-expression** |
| 12th — surrender, the sea | 6th — **service**, the workbench |

**A matured lower house reaches upward on its own.** Self-fulfilment that has
finished growing begins to want partnership; self-love begins to want deep
healing. Where the upward reaching is missing, its absence is itself the
finding.

---

## 4. The six axes share one core

Opposite signs are not opponents. Each pair holds the same core and expresses it
at two ends — a loop circling that core, not two destinations. The opposite is a
**mirror**: the 1st gazes at the 7th.

| axis | shared core |
|---|---|
| Aries – Libra | **Warrior** — power in all its forms: physical, creative, mental, attractive, diplomatic; independence against partnership |
| Taurus – Scorpio | **True Being** — facing one's own shadow and pain until presence needs nothing external |
| Gemini – Sagittarius | **Innocent Play** — discipline and maturity are what let real creativity survive |
| Cancer – Capricorn | **Responsibility** — self-care is the groundwork of contribution, never its opposite |
| Leo – Aquarius | **Social Awareness** — individual development *is* the contribution to the group |
| Virgo – Pisces | **Enlightened Service** — self-compassion first, or the service becomes a tax |

The circuit runs **both ways**: the root generates the reach, and the reach comes
back and strengthens the root.

The axis is not privileged over other aspects. Squares and trines are just as
mathematically telling; they enable friction or ease. Never build a reading that
only looks at oppositions.

---

## 5. Trapped energy, not blocked steps

Where the weight concentrates, charge binds — and everything else runs **below
its potential while still running.**

This is impedance, not a gate. Never write that a capacity is unavailable until
another is finished. Write that it works at a fraction because the charge is
bound somewhere else.

---

## 6. The law: the anchor is across the mirror

**The anchor is the opposite end of wherever the weight sits**, in either
direction. Symmetric.

- **Weight above the horizon** → the work is the lower house facing it, whether
  or not anything stands there. This is the common presenting case, because
  almost every stated want is an upper-house want.
- **Weight below the horizon** → the work is the upper house facing it, and it
  must be **undergone, not understood.** The release is the opposite principle
  actually happening to her, not explained to her.

**Neither half is privileged, and each is privileged differently.** Below the
horizon a person at least knows what she wants, and that consciousness is a real
advantage. Above the horizon the grace is genuine — the 7th-house diplomat is
actually good at it, and it reads to everyone, including her, as a gift.

The performance or envy hidden underneath an upper-house gift is **conditional**:
it requires hard aspects to that gift, *or* a life in which self-fulfilment has
been sacrificed to the relationship every single time. Absent those, the gift is
a gift. Leave it standing.

---

## 7. Point once, at the anchor, and only as a statement

After the mechanism has been described exactly, name the direction — withholding
it is coy. But name it as a fact, never as an assignment.

> Not: *"learn to receive."*
> But: *"The way out is not more competence. It's the sentence you have never
> said out loud, which is that you would like someone to take this from you."*

And point at the **anchor**, never at the destination. She already knows what
she wants; naming the want tells her nothing she did not wake up knowing. What
she has never been told is the unglamorous near-end work that produces it.

---

## 8. The cycle moves regardless, and it is a spiral

The twelve are sequential — a growth chain in which the fulfilment of each step
creates the possibility of the next:

> courage → **self-love** → communication → feeling loved → self-expression →
> humility → equal relationships → **personal transformation** → receiving
> abundance → contributing to others → collective cooperation → surrender

But the wheel turns whether or not anyone is ready: the 12th into the 1st,
Pisces into Aries, March into April. And it is a **spiral, not a circle** — the
same pattern returns at a higher turn, so a missed pass is not fatal and each
return integrates more.

How long a person stays bound in one place is read from the **mode and element
of the sign that naturally governs it.** Fixed earth holds far longer than
mutable air.

---

## 9. The flinch is the honest data

A reader nods along automatically to anything flattering, so agreement is noise
and only refusal is diagnostic. Spend the reading on what she would deny, not on
what she would happily accept.

The same move appears three times in the source course and is one algorithm in
three costumes: the statement you flinch at, the sign whose people you can't
stand, and the upper house you want are all reached by building the anchor in
yourself. The course never once says *go get the thing you want.*

---

## 10. Elements — failure modes

Fire: uninhibited creative expression, energy flowing outward. Earth:
manifestation, grounding the abstract, the present. Air: human connection and
intellectual service, appreciation of differing viewpoints. Water: alignment
through surrender and compassion, ego removed from service.

The source lists three by **surplus** and one by **deficit**, which is worth
preserving:

- Fire excess → self-centredness, inflation
- Earth excess → possessiveness, refusal to move
- Water excess → obsession, victimisation, dependency
- Air **deficiency** → emotional disconnection, depression
